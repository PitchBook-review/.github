Most market research forces you to dig through scattered filings, guess which investors are most active, and jump between disconnected databases. **PitchBook** solves this fragmentation by putting deal measurement, investor sorting, and reporting into one readable environment.

PitchBook solves **one specific problem**: uncertain private market visibility. Just open PitchBook, choose a sector, and see PitchBook deal data with charts and a practical PitchBook overview. If you are tired of guessing which funding rounds, investors, or company profiles are shaping a market, **PitchBook** is for you.
